# For employers

If you came here from my application, thank you. Here you will find the answers to your questions.

## What I am looking for

A role as a **tech lead, solution or AI architect, engineering lead for AI-native development or AI engineer**. I prefer a long-term relationship, ideally **permanent employment**. A long-term contract is also possible.

I can start **from 1 January 2027**, or earlier by agreement. Prague, hybrid.

## What about your own business?

My website and my own projects are my portfolio and the way I stay hands-on with AI. In a permanent role, you get my full commitment: no parallel engagements without your consent, and never a conflict of interest.

## Why I have my own projects

They show how I work better than a CV does. You can try them out and go through their code:

- [Payment matching with AI](/en/projects/payment-matching) – modernising an old Delphi system to .NET with AI, a live demo and code on GitHub.
- [Job Scout](/en/projects/job-scout) – a web application with AI, from design to operations on Azure.
- [Articles](/en/articles) where I describe what works and what doesn’t.

## What I bring

- **I understand the old world and the new one.** Delphi and Oracle PL/SQL as well as .NET, Azure and the cloud. I know why an old system is written the way it is, and how to move it safely.
- **Bringing AI into development in practice.** Spec-driven development with agents, repositories set up for agents, evals and security of AI applications.
- **Leading a team.** For five years I led a development team in the internal IT of VZP ČR, the Czech national health insurer.
- **Understand. Design. Verify.** Today the value isn’t in writing code – AI can do that too – but in making sure the right thing gets built and works.
